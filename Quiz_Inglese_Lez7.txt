QUIZ DI FINE ARGOMENTO · INGLESE TRIENNIO AFM (Prof.ssa Fontanesi) · TRADE AND COMMERCE
Domande in inglese, solo dal materiale della docente. 5 domande per argomento · per continuare almeno l'80%. La risposta giusta è segnata con ✓.

============================================================
Quiz 1 · Trade and commerce
============================================================
1. What does the word 'commerce' refer to?
   ✓ The distribution and sale of physical goods
   – Buying and selling services only
   – The production of raw materials
   – The transport of people
   Spiegazione: With the word 'commerce' we define the distribution and the sale of goods: it is used to talk about physical goods.

2. What is the word 'trade' mostly used for?
   ✓ To talk about buying and selling services
   – To talk about physical shops
   – To talk about taxes
   – To talk about factories
   Spiegazione: Trade refers to buying and selling services; it is mostly used to talk about services.

3. Which factor makes trade and commerce easier?
   ✓ Innovation in technology, transport and communication
   – Fewer contacts between countries
   – Higher border controls
   – The end of trade organisations
   Spiegazione: Innovation in technology, transport and communication systems enables more contact between people and parts of the world.

4. Which of these is a service that supports trade?
   ✓ Insurance
   – Raw materials
   – Electronics
   – Clothes
   Spiegazione: Services to trade include storage, transport, distribution, insurance, banking, marketing and IT support.

5. What are the two types of trade?
   ✓ Home trade and international trade
   – Visible trade and online trade
   – Product trade and money trade
   – Local trade and family trade
   Spiegazione: Trade can be of two different types: home trade and international trade.

============================================================
Quiz 2 · Home trade
============================================================
1. What is home trade?
   ✓ The sale and distribution of products within one country
   – The exchange of goods between countries
   – Selling products only online
   – Producing goods at home
   Spiegazione: Home trade includes the sale and distribution of products within one country, from production to consumption.

2. Who sells goods to retailers in smaller quantities, with a markup?
   ✓ Wholesalers
   – Final consumers
   – Farmers only
   – Banks
   Spiegazione: Wholesalers sell goods to retailers in smaller quantities with a price markup.

3. At what price do final consumers buy goods?
   ✓ At a retail price
   – At a wholesale price
   – At the production cost
   – For free
   Spiegazione: Final consumers buy goods at a retail price.

4. Why are retail sales an indicator of the health of an economy?
   ✓ Consumers spend more in expansion and less in a recession
   – Retailers decide the taxes
   – Retail sales never change
   – Only banks measure them
   Spiegazione: In periods of expansion consumers tend to increase their spending; in periods of recession they spend less.

5. Which is an example of non-store retailing?
   ✓ Door-to-door selling
   – A beauty salon
   – A supermarket
   – A hotel
   Spiegazione: Non-store retailing includes businesses that do not sell from a store: door-to-door selling, distance selling and online selling.

============================================================
Quiz 3 · International trade
============================================================
1. What is international trade?
   ✓ The exchange of goods and services between different countries
   – The sale of products within one country
   – Selling only services online
   – The production of goods for the family
   Spiegazione: International trade is the exchange of goods and services between different countries.

2. What is invisible trade?
   ✓ The exchange of services between countries
   – The exchange of illegal goods
   – Trade without taxes
   – The exchange of raw materials
   Spiegazione: Invisible trade is the exchange of services between countries, such as tourism, insurance and education.

3. Which is a benefit of international trade for consumers?
   ✓ More variety and lower prices
   – Fewer products to choose from
   – Higher prices
   – Less information
   Spiegazione: Thanks to international trade consumers may benefit of more variety and lower prices.

4. What is the balance of trade (BOT)?
   ✓ The difference between total exports and total imports
   – The money of the central bank
   – The number of companies in a country
   – The total of all taxes
   Spiegazione: BOT is the difference between total exports and total imports over a certain period of time.

5. When does a country have a trade deficit?
   ✓ When exports are lower than imports
   – When exports are higher than imports
   – When it has no imports
   – When it pays no taxes
   Spiegazione: We talk about a trade deficit or trade gap when exports are lower than imports.

Quiz 4 · E-commerce
============================================================
1. What does e-commerce refer to?
   ✓ All the aspects of online selling
   – Only shops in the city centre
   – Only the transport of goods
   – Selling products door to door
   Spiegazione: E-commerce refers to all the functional aspects of online selling: website, transactions, payments.

2. Why are consumer habits changing?
   ✓ Because of technology and the presence of smartphones
   – Because shops are cheaper
   – Because the Internet is closing
   – Because people buy less
   Spiegazione: The change is due to the use of technology in everyday life and to the ubiquitous presence of smartphones.

3. What is C2C e-commerce?
   ✓ Sales between consumers, for example on eBay
   – Sales between companies
   – Sales from shops to consumers
   – Sales from consumers to banks
   Spiegazione: C2C is the sale between consumers directly, without a physical shop or a brand.

4. What is O2O commerce?
   ✓ A strategy that turns online visitors into offline sales
   – Shopping only with a smartphone
   – Buying on social media
   – Selling to other companies
   Spiegazione: O2O (online to offline) turns e-commerce visitors into offline sales, for example with click and collect.

5. How did Amazon start in 1994?
   ✓ It only sold books
   – It sold fresh food
   – It offered cloud computing
   – It opened shops with no checkout
   Spiegazione: When Amazon started in 1994, it only sold books.

Quiz 5 · Trade alliances and sanctions
============================================================
1. What is a trade alliance?
   ✓ Countries that create cheaper and freer trade with each other
   – A company that sells abroad
   – A tax on imported goods
   – A ban on trade with a country
   Spiegazione: In a trade alliance, countries of the same economic group create cheaper and freer trade with each other.

2. Which countries are part of the USMCA?
   ✓ USA, Mexico and Canada
   – Brazil, Argentina and Uruguay
   – Italy, France and Spain
   – China, Japan and Korea
   Spiegazione: The USMCA includes the USA, Mexico and Canada; it replaced NAFTA in 2020.

3. What are trade sanctions?
   ✓ Laws and regulations to limit or abolish trade with some countries
   – Agreements to increase trade
   – Discounts on imported goods
   – Prizes for exporters
   Spiegazione: Trade sanctions are laws and regulations to limit or even abolish trade with certain countries.

4. What is an embargo?
   ✓ A total or partial ban on trade with a country
   – A tax on luxury goods
   – A limit on working hours
   – A trade alliance
   Spiegazione: An embargo is a total or partial ban on trade, imposed on specific countries.

5. What does a quota limit?
   ✓ The quantity of a product that can be imported
   – The price of a product
   – The number of shops
   – The salary of workers
   Spiegazione: Quotas are legal restrictions on the quantity of a product that can be imported from abroad.

Quiz 6 · Ethical trade
============================================================
1. What is ethical trade?
   ✓ Improving the working conditions of the people who produce goods
   – Selling goods at the lowest price
   – Trading only with rich countries
   – Producing goods without workers
   Spiegazione: Ethical trade: retailers, brands and suppliers improve the working conditions of the people who produce the goods.

2. Who launched the Ethical Trading Initiative?
   ✓ Companies, NGOs and trade unions
   – Only the government
   – Only consumers
   – Only banks
   Spiegazione: The ETI was launched in the UK in the late 1990s by a group of companies, NGOs and trade unions.

3. What must all ETI members adopt?
   ✓ The Base Code
   – A new logo
   – A minimum price for bananas
   – A trade alliance
   Spiegazione: All ETI members must adopt and enforce the Base Code, an international code of labour practice.

4. Which is a principle of the ETI Base Code?
   ✓ Child labour shall not be used
   – Working hours are always long
   – Wages are as low as possible
   – Workers cannot join unions
   Spiegazione: The Base Code says, for example, that child labour shall not be used and living wages are paid.

5. What does Fairtrade do for banana farmers?
   ✓ It gives them a minimum price and a decent pay
   – It closes their farms
   – It buys bananas at the lowest price
   – It sells only conventional bananas
   Spiegazione: Fairtrade works with banana farmers and workers to create more value and a decent pay; in 2023 it raised the minimum prices.
