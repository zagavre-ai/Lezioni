QUIZ DI FINE ARGOMENTO · INGLESE TRIENNIO AFM (Prof.ssa Fontanesi) · TYPES OF BUSINESS
Domande in inglese, solo dal materiale della docente. 5 domande per argomento · per continuare almeno l'80%. La risposta giusta è segnata con ✓.

============================================================
Quiz 1 · Business structures
============================================================
1. Which business structure is owned, controlled and run by one person?
   ✓ Sole trader
   – Partnership
   – Public limited company
   – Cooperative
   Spiegazione: Sole traders are small businesses owned, controlled and run by one person.

2. What does 'unlimited liability' mean for a sole trader?
   ✓ They are personally responsible for all the debts of the business
   – They can never lose money
   – The debts belong only to the bank
   – The business has no legal obligations
   Spiegazione: Sole traders have unlimited liability: they are personally responsible for all debts incurred in their business.

3. What is a 'sleeping' partner in a limited partnership?
   ✓ A partner who only provides capital
   – A partner who runs the business every day
   – A partner without any investment
   – A partner who is also an employee
   Spiegazione: In limited partnerships, 'sleeping' partners only provide capital.

4. How are profits divided between the shareholders of a limited company?
   ✓ According to the number of shares they hold
   – Equally among all employees
   – According to their age
   – They are never divided
   Spiegazione: Profits are divided between the shareholders based on the number of shares they hold (dividends).

5. How is decision-making usually managed in a cooperative?
   ✓ Democratically: each member has one vote
   – The founder with most money decides
   – The government decides
   – Votes depend on the shares owned
   Spiegazione: Cooperatives are usually run in a democratic way: each member has one vote in decision-making.

============================================================
Quiz 2 · Franchising and start-ups
============================================================
1. Who is the franchisor?
   ✓ The company that sells the right to use its business idea
   – The person who buys the right to copy the business
   – The bank that finances the business
   – The customer of the franchise
   Spiegazione: The franchisor sells the right to use a business idea in a new location.

2. What does a franchisee pay to the franchisor?
   ✓ An initial fee and a percentage of the annual turnover
   – Only a fixed salary
   – Nothing, franchising is free
   – Only the taxes of the franchisor
   Spiegazione: The franchisee pays an initial sum (franchise fee) and a percentage on the annual turnover (management service fee).

3. Why is franchising a low-risk way of starting a business?
   ✓ It uses an already tested and successful business model
   – Because it needs no money at all
   – Because the franchisee can change the model freely
   – Because the government pays the costs
   Spiegazione: Franchising uses an already tested and successful business model, which lowers the possibility of failure.

4. Which is a disadvantage of franchising?
   ✓ Limited flexibility and innovation
   – A weak brand recognition
   – No training or support
   – Difficulty in raising finance from banks
   Spiegazione: The business model cannot be changed by the franchisee, so flexibility and innovation are limited.

5. What is typical of a start-up?
   ✓ High costs and limited financial resources at the beginning
   – A very old and traditional structure
   – Guaranteed success in the first year
   – No need for capital
   Spiegazione: Start-ups begin with high costs and limited financial resources, so they look for capital from several sources.

Quiz 3 · Business growth and multinationals
============================================================
1. What is internal growth?
   ✓ A company uses its own resources to expand
   – A company merges with another one
   – A company is bought by a larger one
   – A company moves abroad
   Spiegazione: In internal growth a company uses its own resources (new assets, new lines of product). It is more gradual.

2. What is vertical integration?
   ✓ Businesses at different stages of production join together
   – Businesses at the same stage join together
   – A company opens a new shop
   – A company reduces its costs
   Spiegazione: Vertical integration: businesses operating at different stages of production join together.

3. What is a takeover (or acquisition)?
   ✓ A business buys another one, entirely or some of its shares
   – Two similar companies form a new one
   – A company closes a department
   – A company becomes non-profit
   Spiegazione: In an acquisition or takeover a business buys another one, either entirely or some shares.

4. When does a parent company control a subsidiary?
   ✓ When it owns or controls more than 50% of the voting shares
   – When it owns 10% of the shares
   – When it has the same name
   – When it is in the same country
   Spiegazione: A parent company either owns or controls more than 50% of the voting share in the subsidiary.

5. Why are multinationals often the reason for debates?
   ✓ Dominance of the market, cheap labour exploitation and tax avoidance
   – They never create jobs
   – They always sell cheaper products
   – They do not use technology
   Spiegazione: Debates are about market dominance, exploitation of cheap labour in developing countries and tax avoidance.

============================================================
Quiz 4 · Internal organisation of a company
============================================================
1. Why does a growing company need an internal organisation?
   ✓ So that everybody understands their role and how to cooperate
   – To pay fewer taxes
   – To avoid having customers
   – To reduce the number of products
   Spiegazione: A company needs an internal organisation so that everybody knows their role and how to cooperate.

2. What does an organigram show?
   ✓ The structure of the departments and the chain of command
   – The profits of the year
   – The list of customers
   – The prices of the products
   Spiegazione: Organigrams graphically represent the structure of the departments and show the chain of command.

3. Who is at the top of an organigram?
   ✓ The Board of Directors and the CEO
   – The office workers
   – The sales representatives
   – The line managers
   Spiegazione: On the top of an organigram you find the Board of Directors and the CEO.

4. What is outsourcing?
   ✓ Contracting work out to external organisations
   – Moving production back to the home country
   – Merging with a competitor
   – Hiring more employees
   Spiegazione: Outsourcing involves contracting work out to external organisations.

5. What is reshoring?
   ✓ A business returns production to the country of origin
   – A business opens a branch abroad
   – A business buys a competitor
   – A business stops production
   Spiegazione: Reshoring is when a business returns production or operations to the country of origin; it is the reverse of offshoring.

Quiz 5 · Corporate Social Responsibility
============================================================
1. What is CSR?
   ✓ The attempt of companies to generate a positive social impact
   – A tax paid to the government
   – A type of bank loan
   – A department that sells products
   Spiegazione: CSR is the attempt of companies to manage their business processes so as to generate a positive social impact.

2. Which of these are stakeholders?
   ✓ Customers, suppliers, employees, shareholders and communities
   – Only the shareholders
   – Only the competitors
   – Only the managers
   Spiegazione: Stakeholders include customers, suppliers, employees, shareholders, communities and the environment.

3. What does 'win-win' mean for CSR?
   ✓ Both society and the company benefit
   – Only the company benefits
   – Only society benefits
   – Nobody benefits
   Spiegazione: CSR is a win-win strategy: it benefits society and the business as well.

4. Which type of CSR is about donating money to worthy causes at local level?
   ✓ Philanthropic responsibility
   – Economic responsibility
   – Environmental responsibility
   – Legal responsibility
   Spiegazione: Philanthropic responsibility: improving society, for example donating money to worthy causes.

5. Which is an advantage of a CSR strategy?
   ✓ Increase in customers' retention and loyalty
   – Higher taxes
   – Less engaged employees
   – A worse public image
   Spiegazione: CSR improves public image, finances, customer loyalty and employees' engagement.
