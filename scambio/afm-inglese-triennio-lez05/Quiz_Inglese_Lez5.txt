Domande in inglese, solo dal materiale della docente. 5 domande per argomento · per continuare almeno l'80%. La risposta giusta è segnata con ✓.

============================================================
Quiz 1 · The concept of marketing
============================================================
1. What characterised the product-oriented model?
   ✓ Companies assumed that high-quality goods would bring success
   – Companies studied the needs of every customer
   – Companies reduced their prices every year
   – Companies sold only services
   Spiegazione: Companies took for granted that their business would be successful if they produced high-quality goods.

2. Why did the model start to change?
   ✓ The number of competitors grew and trade became more global
   – Goods and services became scarce
   – Customers stopped buying products
   – Governments forbade competition
   Spiegazione: With more competitors and an overabundance of goods, the model started to change.

3. What is the priority of the concept of marketing?
   ✓ Customers' satisfaction
   – The lowest production cost
   – The number of employees
   – The size of the factory
   Spiegazione: The concept of marketing considers customers' satisfaction the priority.

4. What is a target market?
   ✓ A particular group of customers
   – The cheapest shop in town
   – A competitor's product
   – A type of price
   Spiegazione: The target market is a particular group of customers chosen in the marketing strategy.

5. What happens in the implementation and control step?
   ✓ Companies monitor results and modify the marketing mix
   – Companies stop the production
   – Companies identify the first opportunity
   – Companies choose a logo
   Spiegazione: After the launch, companies constantly monitor results and modify the marketing mix.

Quiz 2 · Market research
============================================================
1. What is market research?
   ✓ Collecting and analysing information about what people want, need and buy
   – Producing goods at a lower cost
   – Choosing the best logo
   – Selling products online
   Spiegazione: It is the process of collecting and analysing information about what people want, need and buy.

2. What is primary (field) research?
   ✓ Research conducted directly with consumers to collect new information
   – Research using only government reports
   – Research done without customers
   – Research about competitors' logos
   Spiegazione: Primary research is conducted directly with consumers and collects new information (primary data).

3. Which is an example of field research?
   ✓ A focus group
   – A government statistic
   – A newspaper article
   – A bank report
   Spiegazione: Examples of primary research: surveys, consumer panels and focus groups.

4. What is a disadvantage of secondary (desk) research?
   ✓ Data are not always relevant or updated
   – It is very expensive
   – It needs focus groups
   – It collects only new data
   Spiegazione: Desk research is cheaper than field research, but data are not always relevant or updated.

5. What are qualitative data?
   ✓ Descriptive information, such as opinions and beliefs
   – Information expressed only as numbers
   – Charts about population size
   – Test scores
   Spiegazione: Qualitative data are descriptive information which cannot be measured numerically.

Quiz 3 · The marketing strategy (STP)
============================================================
1. What does STP stand for?
   ✓ Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning
   – Selling, Trading and Pricing
   – Strategy, Tactics and Promotion
   – Supply, Technology and Production
   Spiegazione: STP is an acronym for Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning.

2. What is a segment?
   ✓ A smaller group of the market with similar characteristics
   – A type of product
   – A competitor
   – A distribution channel
   Spiegazione: In segmentation the market is divided into smaller groups with similar characteristics.

3. Which criterion answers the question 'where?'
   ✓ Geographic
   – Demographic
   – Psychographic
   – Behavioural
   Spiegazione: Geographic (where?), demographic (who?), psychographic (why?), behavioural (how?).

4. What does targeting involve?
   ✓ Evaluating each segment and selecting the most attractive ones
   – Setting the lowest price
   – Designing a logo
   – Dividing the company into departments
   Spiegazione: Targeting is the evaluation of the commercial attractiveness of each segment and the selection of the target market.

5. What does a market map show?
   ✓ The positions a product can take according to price and quality
   – The shops of a city
   – The employees of a company
   – The history of a brand
   Spiegazione: A market map shows the positions a product can take on the market according to price and quality.

Quiz 4 · The marketing mix
============================================================
1. What are the 4Ps of marketing?
   ✓ Product, price, promotion, place
   – People, process, product, price
   – Plan, produce, pack, post
   – Profit, power, position, publicity
   Spiegazione: The 4Ps are product, price, promotion and place.

2. What are Unique Selling Points (USP)?
   ✓ Features that make a product different, like design or colour
   – The lowest prices on the market
   – The shops of a franchise
   – The customers of a brand
   Spiegazione: Manufacturers differentiate products through features that become their Unique Selling Points.

3. Which two kinds of factors influence the price?
   ✓ Internal and external factors
   – Local and global factors
   – Social and ethical factors
   – Digital and physical factors
   Spiegazione: Price is set by considering internal factors (production cost) and external factors (competing prices).

4. What does 'promotion' mean in the marketing mix?
   ✓ How customers are informed about products
   – Where products are sold
   – How much products cost
   – How products are made
   Spiegazione: Promotion is the process of making a business and its products known to potential customers.

5. Which are the three extra Ps of the 7Ps?
   ✓ Physical evidence, process and people
   – Profit, plan and power
   – Packaging, price and position
   – Promotion, place and product
   Spiegazione: The extended marketing mix adds physical evidence, process and people.

Quiz 5 · Branding and pricing
============================================================
1. What is branding?
   ✓ A technique to differentiate products from competitors' ones
   – A way to reduce production costs
   – A type of market research
   – A tax for companies
   Spiegazione: Branding is a technique used by companies to differentiate their products from competitors' ones.

2. Which is an example of a brand name that is an invented word?
   ✓ Google
   – McDonald's
   – General Motors
   – American Airlines
   Spiegazione: Invented words: Google. Family names: McDonald's. Descriptive: General Motors. Geographical: American Airlines.

3. What are own-brand products?
   ✓ Products that supermarkets market under their own name
   – Products without a logo
   – Products made by the customer
   – Products sold only online
   Spiegazione: Supermarket chains buy products from manufacturers and market them under their name or a new brand name.

4. What is a penetration price?
   ✓ A low price to enter a new market and gain market share
   – A very high price for exclusivity
   – A price just below a round number
   – A price reduced for one year
   Spiegazione: Penetration price: offering a low price to enter a new market and/or gain market share.

5. What is an odd (psychological) price?
   ✓ A price just below a round number, like 9,99
   – A price much higher than competitors
   – A temporary discount
   – A price fixed by the government
   Spiegazione: Odd or psychological prices create the perception of a good deal (9,99 instead of 10).

Quiz 6 · Digital marketing
============================================================
1. Why do companies need digital marketing strategies?
   ✓ To reach target customers on the platforms they use most
   – To avoid using the Internet
   – To reduce the number of customers
   – To stop advertising
   Spiegazione: Companies need to reach their target customers on the platforms they use the most.

2. What is Content Marketing?
   ✓ Online material that sparks interest without directly promoting a brand
   – Sending newsletters only
   – Paying for search results
   – Selling products in shops
   Spiegazione: Content marketing creates online material that doesn't directly promote a brand but sparks interest in its products.

3. What does SEO stand for?
   ✓ Search Engine Optimisation
   – Social Engagement Operations
   – Sales and Export Office
   – Secure Email Organisation
   Spiegazione: Search Engine Optimisation: most customers use searches to find products and services.

4. Which is a challenge of digital marketing?
   ✓ Complying with privacy and data sharing regulations
   – Having too few customers
   – Printing catalogues
   – Opening physical shops
   Spiegazione: Companies need to comply with privacy laws, some of them transnational (GDPR).

5. What is an omnichannel strategy?
   ✓ Using email, social media, website, SEO and newsletters together
   – Using only one social network
   – Selling only in physical shops
   – Avoiding mobile devices
   Spiegazione: Omnichannel strategies include email, social media, website, SEO and newsletters.
