Domande in inglese, solo dal materiale della docente. 5 domande per argomento · per continuare almeno l'80%. La risposta giusta è segnata con ✓.

============================================================
Quiz 1 · Today's job market
============================================================
1. What is the job market?
   ✓ The market where businesses that need employees and people looking for work interact
   – The market where companies sell their products
   – A place where people buy shares
   – A list of open schools
   Spiegazione: Job market: the market where there is an interaction between businesses that need employees and individuals who are looking for work.

2. What is a tight job market?
   ✓ There are many job offers but few people looking for work
   – There are more people looking for work than jobs
   – There are no job offers at all
   – Wages are always very low
   Spiegazione: Tight: there are many job offers but few people looking for work.

3. What happens during recessions?
   ✓ Unemployment rises and wages are low
   – Businesses compete for workers
   – Wages increase quickly
   – There are no unemployed people
   Spiegazione: During recessions, unemployment rises and there is economic stagnation, with a low level of wages.

4. What do many employers prefer today?
   ✓ People with the right skills, rather than only qualifications
   – Only people with many years of experience
   – Only people with a university degree
   – People who do not want to learn
   Spiegazione: Many employers prefer hiring people with the necessary skills to perform current tasks and adapt rapidly to new challenges.

5. Why are there many job vacancies?
   ✓ Ageing population, less immigration and fewer people accepting low-wage jobs
   – There are too many workers
   – Companies are closing
   – Wages are too high
   Spiegazione: This situation is due to an ageing population, a decline in immigration and a change in attitude towards work.

============================================================
Quiz 2 · Job stability
============================================================
1. How was work in the past?
   ✓ People had long-term stable jobs with the same employer
   – People changed job every month
   – Everybody was a freelancer
   – Nobody had a contract
   Spiegazione: In the past people had long-term stable jobs with the same employer.

2. What is the gig economy?
   ✓ Jobs that last a specific length of time, like freelance work
   – Jobs in the music industry only
   – Jobs for life in big companies
   – Jobs in the public sector
   Spiegazione: The gig economy refers to jobs lasting a specific length of time: freelancers, independent contractors, temporary hires.

3. Which is an example of a gig job without specific qualifications?
   ✓ Food delivery
   – Digital marketer
   – Freelance writer
   – Engineer
   Spiegazione: It ranges from professional services to jobs that do not require specific qualifications, like food delivery services.

4. Which is an advantage of gig work?
   ✓ Flexibility and a better work-life balance
   – A high minimum wage
   – Free health insurance
   – No taxes to pay
   Spiegazione: Gig workers can choose the types of jobs they do, when and where they do them: work-life balance.

5. Which is a disadvantage of gig work?
   ✓ Modest pay and no benefits
   – Too much stability
   – Too many holidays
   – A fixed salary for life
   Spiegazione: Gig workers don't have the protection of minimum wages, health insurance and trade unions.

============================================================
Quiz 3 · Flexibility at work
============================================================
1. What made it possible to work anywhere and at any time?
   ✓ Digital devices
   – Longer holidays
   – New factories
   – Paper documents
   Spiegazione: Thanks to digital devices, it is possible to work anywhere and at any time.

2. What changed the vision of remote working?
   ✓ The Covid-19 pandemic
   – A new law on holidays
   – The invention of the telephone
   – The end of the Internet
   Spiegazione: The Covid-19 pandemic completely changed this vision: flexible work patterns are now the norm.

3. What is job sharing?
   ✓ Two people share one full-time job, each working part-time
   – One person does two jobs
   – A team shares an office
   – Workers share their salaries
   Spiegazione: Job sharing: when two people share one full-time job, each one working part-time.

4. What is a condensed work week?
   ✓ Working longer hours on fewer days
   – Working fewer hours every day
   – Working only at night
   – Working seven days a week
   Spiegazione: Condensed work week: for example, four days of 10 hours instead of five days of 8 hours.

5. What do almost half of Gen Z employees prefer?
   ✓ A hybrid model, combining remote and office work
   – Full remote work
   – Only office work
   – Working at night
   Spiegazione: Almost half of them prefer a hybrid model; full remote work may make them feel isolated.

Quiz 4 · Digital communication
============================================================
1. Where does digital communication take place?
   ✓ Internally among staff and externally with customers
   – Only inside the office
   – Only with suppliers
   – Only on paper
   Spiegazione: It takes place both internally among staff and externally with customers.

2. Which is a main form of digital communication?
   ✓ Video conferencing
   – Letters by post
   – Fax only
   – Notice boards
   Spiegazione: Its main forms are emails, instant messaging, live chat, video conferencing and project management tools.

3. What is the main feature of digital communication?
   ✓ Information is transmitted instantaneously
   – It is very expensive
   – It is slower than letters
   – It works only in one country
   Spiegazione: The main feature of digital communication is that information is transmitted instantaneously.

4. Who invented the Zoom app in 2011?
   ✓ Eric Yuan
   – Bill Gates
   – Steve Jobs
   – Jeff Bezos
   Spiegazione: The Zoom app was invented in 2011 by Eric Yuan, a former vice president of Cisco.

5. What led to Zoom's explosive growth?
   ✓ The Covid-19 pandemic
   – A new smartphone
   – A football championship
   – A drop in prices
   Spiegazione: It was the Covid-19 pandemic that led to the company's explosive growth.

Quiz 5 · Employability skills
============================================================
1. What are employability skills?
   ✓ Qualities that make a person attractive for employment
   – Only university degrees
   – Only computer programs
   – Only years of experience
   Spiegazione: Employability skills: qualities and attributes making an individual attractive for employment and successful in the workplace.

2. What is the advantage of soft skills compared to hard skills?
   ✓ They can be applied in real-world situations
   – They are learnt only at school
   – They never change
   – They are only technical
   Spiegazione: Their main advantage is that they can be applied in practical circumstances and in real-world situations.

3. Which are the three types of employability skills?
   ✓ How you work, how you work with others, how you think
   – How you eat, sleep and study
   – How you dress, speak and walk
   – How you buy, sell and save
   Spiegazione: They can be divided into three types: how you work, how you work with others, how you think.

4. What does 'working to deadlines' mean?
   ✓ Prioritising and planning, being reliable
   – Working only at night
   – Never finishing work
   – Working without a plan
   Spiegazione: Working to deadlines: prioritizing and planning, reliability.

5. What does negotiation mean?
   ✓ Looking for ideas that benefit everybody: give and take
   – Winning every discussion
   – Never accepting other ideas
   – Working alone
   Spiegazione: Negotiation: looking for ideas benefitting all, 'give and take'.
