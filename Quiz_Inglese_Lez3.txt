============================================================
Quiz 1 · Production and sectors of production
============================================================
1. What is production?
   ✓ The process of making goods and providing services
   – The process of selling goods at a lower price
   – The process of saving money in a bank
   – The process of buying raw materials only
   Spiegazione: Production is the process of making goods and providing services.

2. Which of these is an example of non-durable goods?
   ✓ Food
   – Cars
   – Furniture
   – Education
   Spiegazione: Non-durable goods are consumed once or used in a short period of time, like food.

3. Which factor of production means human skills and work effort?
   ✓ Labour
   – Land
   – Capital
   – Entrepreneurship
   Spiegazione: Labour: human skills and work effort.

4. Which sector converts raw materials into semi-finished or finished goods?
   ✓ The secondary sector
   – The primary sector
   – The tertiary sector
   – The quinary sector
   Spiegazione: The secondary sector converts raw materials into semi-finished or finished goods (manufacturing, building, clothing, food).

5. What is responsible sourcing?
   ✓ Considering social and environmental responsibilities when managing suppliers
   – Buying raw materials always at the lowest price
   – Producing everything inside one country
   – Stopping all relationships with suppliers
   Spiegazione: Responsible sourcing means that companies consider their social and environmental responsibilities when managing relationships with suppliers.

============================================================
Quiz 2 · Economies of scale
============================================================
1. What happens to the unit cost when a business expands its scale of production?
   ✓ It decreases
   – It increases
   – It stays exactly the same
   – It disappears completely
   Spiegazione: When a business expands its scale of production, there is a decrease in the unit cost.

2. Why do fixed costs help to create economies of scale?
   ✓ They are distributed across a higher number of units
   – They grow with every unit produced
   – They are paid only by small businesses
   – They are paid by the government
   Spiegazione: Fixed costs, like rent and administration, are distributed across a higher number of units.

3. Buying in bulk is an example of…
   ✓ an internal economy of scale
   – an external economy of scale
   – a diseconomy of scale
   – offshoring
   Spiegazione: Internal economies of scale come from the business itself: buying in bulk means a lower price per item.

4. Similar companies in the same geographical area, like the Ceramic Valley, are an example of…
   ✓ clustering
   – division of labour
   – reshoring
   – quality control
   Spiegazione: Clustering is an external economy of scale: similar companies in the same geographical area.

5. Which of these is a way to solve diseconomies of scale?
   ✓ Outsourcing certain processes to third-party providers
   – Hiring more managers without a plan
   – Making administrative processes slower
   – Reducing communication between workers
   Spiegazione: Diseconomies of scale can be solved with more efficient production, leaner administration and outsourcing.

Quiz 3 · Quality control and smart manufacturing
============================================================
1. What does quality control (QC) ensure?
   ✓ That products meet standards of quality and safety before they are sold
   – That products are always the cheapest on the market
   – That products are sold only abroad
   – That workers do not need training
   Spiegazione: QC ensures that products meet certain standards of quality and safety before they are sold to the public.

2. Which of these is typically part of quality control?
   ✓ Visual inspections and testing of materials
   – Moving the factory abroad
   – Increasing the price of products
   – Buying in bulk
   Spiegazione: QC typically consists of visual inspections, testing of materials and equipment to detect defects.

3. Smart manufacturing is typical of…
   ✓ Industry 4.0
   – the linear economy
   – the primary sector
   – the post-war period
   Spiegazione: Smart manufacturing is typical of Industry 4.0, where machines and production systems are interconnected.

4. What do collaborative robots show?
   ✓ Human-machine interaction
   – Clustering
   – Responsible sourcing
   – Production offshoring
   Spiegazione: Human-machine interaction: people and automated systems work together to achieve shared goals (e.g. collaborative robots).

5. What is a digital twin?
   ✓ A virtual replica used for testing
   – A second factory in another country
   – A copy of a contract
   – A worker who does two jobs
   Spiegazione: Augmented reality can create digital twins, virtual replicas for testing purposes.

Quiz 4 · Relocation of business operations
============================================================
1. Why do large companies move some production activities to other countries?
   ✓ For lower labour costs and easier access to resources
   – To pay higher taxes
   – To be further from their target markets
   – To increase shipping costs
   Spiegazione: Companies relocate for easier access to resources, lower labour costs, environmental regulations and market access.

2. What is offshoring?
   ✓ Relocating business processes to different international locations due to lower costs
   – Returning production to the country of origin
   – Selling products only online
   – Closing a business forever
   Spiegazione: Offshoring: relocating business processes or operations to different international locations due to lower costs.

3. Moving customer service to another country is an example of…
   ✓ service offshoring
   – production offshoring
   – reshoring
   – clustering
   Spiegazione: Service offshoring is the relocation of support processes, like IT and customer service.

4. How is outsourcing different from offshoring?
   ✓ Outsourcing uses external organisations, but not necessarily in another country
   – Outsourcing always happens in another country
   – Outsourcing means returning home
   – There is no difference
   Spiegazione: Outsourcing involves contracting work out to external organisations, but it doesn't have to be in another country.

5. What is reshoring?
   ✓ When a business returns production to the country of origin
   – When a business moves production abroad
   – When a business buys in bulk
   – When a business recycles materials
   Spiegazione: Reshoring: when a business returns production or operations to the country of origin. It is the reverse of offshoring.

============================================================
Quiz 5 · Focus: the circular economy
============================================================
1. What does the circular economy consist in?
   ✓ Reusing and recycling materials to reduce consumption and waste
   – Extracting more raw materials
   – Throwing products away after one use
   – Producing only in one country
   Spiegazione: The circular economy consists in reusing and recycling materials to reduce consumption of raw materials and minimise waste and pollution.

2. In the linear economy, raw materials are…
   ✓ extracted, used and then thrown away
   – always reused and repaired
   – returned to the Earth
   – never extracted
   Spiegazione: In the linear economy raw materials are extracted, used and then thrown away.

3. The circular economy is an answer to which problems?
   ✓ Climate change, waste and pollution
   – High taxes and low salaries
   – Bad marketing and low sales
   – Small factories and few workers
   Spiegazione: Circular economy is an answer to the problems of climate change, waste and pollution.

4. In the technical cycle, products last longer thanks to…
   ✓ reusing, repairing and recycling
   – buying new products more often
   – moving factories abroad
   – using more raw materials
   Spiegazione: Technical cycle: products and materials last longer thanks to reusing, repairing and recycling.

5. What happens in the biological cycle?
   ✓ Nutrients from biodegradable materials are returned to the Earth
   – Products are repaired in factories
   – Plastic is burned for energy
   – Materials are sent abroad
   Spiegazione: Biological cycle: the nutrients from biodegradable materials are returned to the Earth.
