QUIZ DI FINE ARGOMENTO · INGLESE (Amministrazione, Finanza e Marketing) · Prof.ssa Maria Vittoria Fontanesi
Global Factors · 5 quiz da 5 domande · per continuare almeno l’80% (4 risposte giuste su 5).
La risposta giusta è segnata con ✓. Sotto ogni domanda: la spiegazione (frase della dispensa).

============================================================
Quiz 1 · Globalisation
============================================================
1. How can globalisation be defined?
   ✓ The increasing level of integration of the world we live in
   – The decreasing role of international trade
   – The independence of national economies
   – The protection of local markets from foreign products
   Spiegazione: Globalisation can be defined as the increasing level of integration of the world we live in.

2. In business terms, globalisation has to do with…
   ✓ the increasing interdependence of world economies
   – the closing of national borders to trade
   – the reduction of multinational corporations
   – the end of international investments
   Spiegazione: In business terms, globalisation has to do with the increasing interdependence of world economies.

3. Which of these is one of the most visible effects of globalisation?
   ✓ A rise in the number of multinational corporations
   – A decrease in international trade
   – Fewer goods exchanged between countries
   – Less movement of capital
   Spiegazione: The effects are free movement of capital, goods and services, development of international trade and a rise in the number of multinational corporations.

4. Why did transportation help globalisation?
   ✓ Larger, faster ships and economies of scale made each item cheaper
   – Ships became smaller and slower
   – Transport was banned between continents
   – Goods could only travel by air
   Spiegazione: Larger ships and speed of transport, plus economies of scale: the cost per item is lower for large quantities.

5. Why do many companies produce in LDCs (Least Developed Countries)?
   ✓ Less regulation and lower labour costs
   – Higher taxes and stricter rules
   – No workers are available there
   – Products cannot be exported from there
   Spiegazione: Labour availability in LDCs: less regulation and lower labour costs.

============================================================
Quiz 2 · Technological advancement
============================================================
1. Why does technological advancement have a deep impact on business?
   ✓ New products arrive and goods are produced and delivered in better and faster ways
   – It makes all products more expensive
   – It stops companies from producing new goods
   – It only affects the entertainment industry
   Spiegazione: While new products are introduced on the market, also better and faster ways of producing and delivering goods are developed.

2. What are smart factories?
   ✓ Plants that are more efficient, more productive and less wasteful
   – Factories run only by hand
   – Small family workshops
   – Factories without any machines
   Spiegazione: Smart factories are plants that are more efficient and productive, and less wasteful.

3. What is the Internet of Things (IoT)?
   ✓ Machines with sensors that connect and exchange data with other devices
   – A social media platform for factories
   – A type of mobile phone
   – A list of websites for businesses
   Spiegazione: In smart factories machines have sensors allowing them to connect and exchange data with other devices.

4. What is a Digital Twin?
   ✓ A virtual replica of a process, a production line or a supply chain
   – A second factory built next to the first
   – A copy of a worker's identity card
   – A twin brother who works in the same company
   Spiegazione: Digital Twin: virtual replicas of processes, lines of production and supply chains, used to increase productivity and test changes.

5. What is the difference between edge computing and cloud computing?
   ✓ Edge computing processes data on the user's own device; cloud computing delivers services over the internet
   – They are exactly the same thing
   – Edge computing uses only paper archives
   – Cloud computing works only without internet
   Spiegazione: Edge computing: local processing on the user's own device. Cloud computing: delivery of services over the internet.

Quiz 3 · Diversity and inclusion
============================================================
1. Which factors changed society and its attitude towards diversity?
   ✓ Globalisation, technological development and migration
   – Only the weather
   – Only the end of the pandemic
   – The closing of borders
   Spiegazione: Society has radically changed due to globalisation, technological development and migration.

2. What do many people believe today about opportunities?
   ✓ Everyone should have equal access to them, regardless of their conditions
   – Only some groups should have them
   – Opportunities depend only on age
   – Opportunities should be decided by chance
   Spiegazione: People believe everyone should have equal access to opportunities, regardless of gender, age, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation and ability.

3. Which of these conditions is mentioned in the handout?
   ✓ Mental and physical ability
   – Favourite sport
   – Shoe size
   – Mobile phone brand
   Spiegazione: The conditions listed are gender, age, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, mental and physical ability.

4. Who is "GenZ"?
   ✓ The young people now looking for a job
   – Retired workers
   – Company managers over 60
   – Primary school children
   Spiegazione: The majority of young people looking for a job (GenZ) consider diversity and inclusion important.

5. What do businesses need to do to attract and retain young talents?
   ✓ Rethink their recruitment policies and value everyone's contribution
   – Reduce all salaries
   – Hire only people from one country
   – Stop recruiting young people
   Spiegazione: Businesses need to rethink their recruitment policies and the way they include and value everyone's contribution.

Quiz 4 · World instability
============================================================
1. Since when has a wave of global instability been going on?
   ✓ Since 2019
   – Since 1989
   – Since 2010
   – Since 2024
   Spiegazione: A wave of instability on a global level has been going since 2019.

2. Which was a disruptive effect of the pandemic on the economy?
   ✓ Shortages in labour and delays in raw materials and deliveries
   – Lower prices for all transport
   – More workers than jobs everywhere
   – Faster deliveries of finished goods
   Spiegazione: There were shortages in labour, logistical bottlenecks, and delays in raw materials and finished goods.

3. What was the most visible effect of the war in Ukraine on countries not directly involved?
   ✓ Inflationary pressure, with higher transport costs and raw material prices
   – A fall in all prices
   – Lower transport costs
   – An increase in investor confidence
   Spiegazione: The most visible effect was the inflationary pressure: higher transportation costs and higher prices of raw materials.

4. According to the European Central Bank survey, what did the pandemic transform?
   ✓ The way businesses operate and the behaviour of customers
   – Only the opening hours of shops
   – Only the colours of products
   – Nothing in the long term
   Spiegazione: The survey showed the pandemic transformed both the way businesses operate and the general behaviour of customers.

5. When did the global Covid-19 pandemic end, according to the handout?
   ✓ In 2022
   – In 2019
   – In 2020
   – In 2025
   Spiegazione: With the end of the global pandemic of Covid-19 in 2022, many countries abandoned lockdowns and travel restrictions.

Quiz 5 · Environmental concerns
============================================================
1. In how many ways does climate change influence business, according to the handout?
   ✓ 6
   – 2
   – 4
   – 10
   Spiegazione: Climate change influences business in 6 ways.

2. Why does climate change increase the risk for businesses?
   ✓ It makes extreme weather events like floods and droughts more likely
   – It reduces the number of customers to zero
   – It makes all products cheaper
   – It stops international trade
   Spiegazione: Climate change increases the likelihood of extreme weather events, which can damage businesses.

3. What happens when resources become scarce because of global warming?
   ✓ Their price tends to increase
   – Their price tends to fall
   – They become free
   – Nothing changes
   Spiegazione: If resources are scarce, their price tends to increase.

4. What do most customers prefer, because of public pressure?
   ✓ Products and services produced sustainably
   – The cheapest products, whatever their impact
   – Products made with more CO2
   – Products from companies that pollute
   Spiegazione: Most customers prefer products and services sustainably produced by companies active in reducing their environmental impact.

5. Which example of more difficult working conditions does the handout give?
   ✓ Working outdoors in extremely hot weather
   – Working from home on a computer
   – Working fewer hours
   – Working in an air-conditioned office
   Spiegazione: Working conditions tend to worsen, for example working outdoors in extremely hot weather.
